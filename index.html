How we estimate

A simple, open model. The pilot replaces it with the station's own meter and sales data.

Savings use today's gow cashback (5 c/L on fuel, 5 c per € in the shop, 2 c/kWh on charging), doubled on greener moves.

To be honest about scale: the fuel sold at a busy station emits roughly 28 t of CO₂e a day (about 3.5 million litres a year, well-to-wheel). These moves are small next to that. Moves that replace a petrol trip weigh most, which is why they earn most.

Why the public numbers are smooth

The big screen shows totals for all of Spain, as a 30-day average, rounded and published a day or two later. The counter climbs at one steady speed, so nobody can read this station's traffic from it: not the person behind you, and not a competitor. A station only ever shows shares and an index. Your exact numbers stay on your phone.
