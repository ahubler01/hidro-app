Meet Hidro.
Every move, it grows.

A clickable mock-up of Hidro in the Moeve gow app. Click through it like a real phone.

  1. Home: your Hidro, plus what you have saved and avoided. Only you see this.
  2. Pay: tick what you did and scan your QR code. Each move shows the euros it saves and the CO₂e it avoids.
  3. The Plaza: the station's big screen. The community shrinks the station's daily CO₂ cloud.
  4. Simulate a move: trigger a coffee, an EV charge or a tyre check and watch the screen react.
Privacy: the big screen updates only every 5 scans and shows 30-day averages, so nobody behind you can see what you did.
Honesty: the screen also says that the fuel sold here emits about 28 t of CO₂e a day.

How we estimate

A simple, open model the pilot can replace with the station's real meter and sales data.

1 · The station's own footprint (scope 1 + 2)

Electricity for pumps, lights, shop fridges, coffee machines and car wash: ~160 MWh a year × 0.12 kg CO₂e/kWh (Spanish grid)19 t
Refrigerant leaks and heating6 t
Total ≈ 25 t a year69 kg/day

This is the cloud on the big screen. Every day it starts full, and green moves shrink it. Hitting 100% means the community avoided as much CO₂e as the station itself emitted that day.

2 · The fuel sold here (scope 3)

~3.5 million litres a year × ~2.9 kg CO₂e/L (well-to-wheel)≈ 28 t/day

We show this on screen for honesty. It's far bigger than the station's own footprint, which is why moves that replace a petrol trip weigh most.

3 · What each move avoids

4 · Savings

Current gow cashback (5c/L fuel, 5c per € in the shop, 2c/kWh charging) plus Hidro's double points on charging and the shop. Fuel saves money but never feeds Hidro.

5 · Privacy on the big screen

Totals update only after every 5 scans, and the per-member figure is a 30-day rolling average. Nobody behind you can work out what you did.
